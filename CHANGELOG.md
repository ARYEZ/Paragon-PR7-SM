# Changelog

## 0.2.2

- The family list (`family.py`) knows the four newest Paragon apps: PR7-HX (hex editor), PR7-DB (SQLite browser),
  PR7-UN (uninstaller) and PR7-LN (launcher), so links between the apps and the PR7-U offer find them.

## 0.2.1

- The family's list knows the newest apps (PR7-HB, PR7-FS, PR7-AR, PR7-WM), so links between apps and Check for Updates find them.

## 0.2.0

- **One finish for the whole family.** Choosing OBSIDIAN or AURORA here switches every Paragon app, open ones included (within a couple of seconds), and every app starts in the family's choice. PR7-SM's menu has a Finish choice now; its lower pane is light in AURORA (it stayed dark), and new processes show in AURORA too.
- In AURORA, group titles and selected rows are dark and readable (they were white on the light finish).

## 0.1.0

- First release: a system monitor that works the way Process Explorer works. The process tree refreshed every second
  with CPU, private bytes, working set, description and company; Process Explorer's row colours in the family's;
  sort by any column; the DLLs and handles of the selected process; kill, kill tree, suspend, set priority;
  Properties with live graphs; System Information; Find a handle or DLL; Replace Task Manager. It updates itself like
  the other family apps.
